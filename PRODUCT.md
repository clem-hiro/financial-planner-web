# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Singapore-based individuals and advisor-linked clients who maintain a private financial tracker for income, cash flow, CPF, property, vehicles, liabilities, goals, and retirement planning. Users are usually completing or reviewing structured setup data and need to understand financial position without feeling like they are in a spreadsheet or admin console.

## Product Purpose

Finance Planner helps users keep a canonical personal finance record and turn it into clear planning views for cash flow, wealth, protection, and long-horizon decisions. Success means the app makes trade-offs, projections, shortfalls, and assumptions explicit while keeping data entry calm, recoverable, and reviewable.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a premium private wealth operating system: focused on clarity, progressive disclosure, and explicit methodology rather than noisy consumer fintech engagement patterns.

## Anti-references

Avoid spreadsheet-like density, accounting-package chrome, crowded dashboards, heavy borders, over-tabbed navigation, gamified consumer-fintech visuals, and black-box scoring. Do not bury assumptions or make source-owned financial records feel duplicated across setup sections.

## Design Principles

1. Show the current financial position first, then reveal assumptions and edit controls progressively.
2. Keep canonical setup data trustworthy by making source ownership, redirects, and duplicate prevention visible.
3. Preserve calm information density: compact summaries by default, deeper detail on demand.
4. Make debt, cash flow, CPF, housing, and vehicle semantics explicit enough for Singapore planning without overwhelming the first view.
5. Treat advisor collaboration as transparent suggestions, not silent edits to client data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use accessible native controls where possible, including keyboard-operable disclosure panels and form controls with labels. Maintain readable contrast, avoid color-only status signals, support reduced-motion preferences, and keep layouts responsive for mobile setup workflows.
