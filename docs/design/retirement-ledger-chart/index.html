Retirement runway — ledger chart

Two concrete directions for the chart that visualizes the new retirement cash-flow ledger (retirement-cashflow-projection.ts). Both are calm, premium, minimal and fully responsive (web + mobile). Each file has a Desktop / Mobile preview toggle at the top.

The one decision to make first: what does a bar mean?

Both proposals draw the same milestones, dual axis, net-worth line and collapsible pane. They differ only in the bar semantics, which is a real modeling choice:

ReadingBar heightWhere shortfall showsBest when you want users to feel…
A · funded spending That year's spending (requiredOutflow), segmented by funder Terracotta cap on top of the bar (uncovered spending) “Every year of my life is paid for — here's by what.”
B · income by source That year's total income (cashAccessibleInflow), stacked by source; spending is a dashed line Terracotta fills the gap from the income top up to the spending line “My income vs my spending — and exactly when one crosses the other.”
